DATA ACCESS NOTICE

The data that support the findings of this study are available from the
Ethics Committee Research (EC Research) UZ/KU Leuven, but restrictions apply
to the availability of these data, which were used under licence for the
current study, and so are not publicly available. Data are however available
from the authors upon reasonable request and with permission of EC
Research UZ/KU Leuven.

The public repository therefore does not distribute the patient-level cohort
files originally used by the code.

If approved access is obtained, the code expects local copies with these names:
- data as published Amicool.xlsx
- dataset as published creacool.xlsx
- NONMEM.xlsx

Clinical characteristics of the three datasets used are summarized in Table 7,
"Clinical characteristics of patients in the three neonatal cohorts," in the
paper.
